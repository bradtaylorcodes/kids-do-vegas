# Kids Do Vegas

A public directory of things for kids (ages 0–18) and families to do and use across the Las Vegas Valley: ongoing resources, dated events, registration-based programs, and the organizations and locations behind them.

## Language

### Listings

**Listing**:
Any Resource, Event, or Program shown to visitors. Used only when speaking about all three together.
_Avoid_: Item, post, entry

**Resource**:
An ongoing offering for kids or families that is not tied to a date, such as a park, a museum, a pediatric dentist, or free grocery distribution. A recurring service schedule ("2nd Saturday, 9–11am") does not make it an Event.
_Avoid_: Place, service, amenity

**Event**:
A dated occurrence a family would plan an outing around, either one-off or recurring (e.g. weekly storytime). May belong to a Resource and/or an Organization.
_Avoid_: Activity, happening

**Recurring Event**:
An Event defined by a repeating schedule that produces individual dated occurrences on the calendar.
_Avoid_: Series, repeating event

**Special Event**:
An Event that is one-off or occasional, the reason to check the calendar this weekend. Events are Special unless marked Regular.
_Avoid_: Featured event, highlight

**Regular**:
An Event that happens routinely and frequently, such as storytime or open swim, shown apart from Special Events so it does not crowd them out.
_Avoid_: Routine event, weekly event, recurring event (a Regular need not be stored as a Recurring Event)

**Program**:
An offering with a defined run that requires registration, such as an 8-week swim class or a summer camp.
_Avoid_: Class, camp, course (these are kinds of Program, not separate concepts)

**Category**:
The kind of Resource, such as Parks, Museums, Healthcare, or Food Assistance, used for browsing.
_Avoid_: Type, tag

### Cost

**Cost Tier**:
How much a Listing costs, as one of Free, Low Cost, or Paid. Free is a top-level filter.
_Avoid_: Price level, pricing

**Price Note**:
Short free text giving price details, such as "$5 per child, under 2 free".
_Avoid_: Price, fee

### Who and where

**Organization**:
The entity that provides Resources, Events, or Programs, such as the library district, Three Square, or a swim school.
_Avoid_: Provider, vendor, host, nonprofit

**Location**:
A physical address within an Area where a Listing happens. A Resource may have zero, one, or many Locations.
_Avoid_: Place, venue, address

### Audience

**Age Range**:
The minimum and maximum child age a Listing is suitable for, from 0 to 18.
_Avoid_: Ages, audience

**Age Group**:
A named band of ages used for filtering: Baby (0–1), Toddler (1–3), Preschool (3–5), Elementary (5–10), Tween (10–13), Teen (13–18). A Listing matches every Age Group its Age Range overlaps.
_Avoid_: Age bracket, level

**All Ages**:
A Listing whose Age Range covers 0–18.
_Avoid_: Family-friendly (as an age label)

### Geography

**Las Vegas Valley**:
The service area: the City of Las Vegas, Henderson, North Las Vegas, Boulder City, and unincorporated Clark County (Summerlin, Enterprise, Spring Valley, etc.).
_Avoid_: Las Vegas (when meaning the whole region), Vegas

**Area**:
A named sub-region of the Las Vegas Valley used for browsing and filtering, such as Henderson, Summerlin, or Northwest. Day-trip destinations outside the valley belong to a dedicated "Day Trips" Area.
_Avoid_: Neighborhood, zone, region

### Data

**Source**:
Where a Listing's data came from: entered by hand, collected from a named feed (e.g. the library district calendar), or submitted by an Organization.
_Avoid_: Origin, provider

**Collector**:
An automated job that pulls Listings from one external Source on a schedule and keeps them up to date. A Collector only keeps Listings whose Age Range falls within 0–18.
_Avoid_: Scraper, crawler, importer, sync

**Last Verified**:
The date a person last confirmed a hand-entered Listing is still accurate. Shown to visitors.
_Avoid_: Updated at, freshness

**Needs Verification**:
A hand-entered Listing whose Last Verified date is more than 6 months old. It stays visible to visitors.
_Avoid_: Stale, expired, outdated

**Problem Report**:
A visitor's flag that a Listing is wrong or closed, sent to the editor for review.
_Avoid_: Complaint, feedback, flag
